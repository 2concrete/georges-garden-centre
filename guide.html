Easy to follow gardening quickstart guide

Guideing you through your gardening journey

  • 1. Pick a Spot

    Choose a place with 6 to 8 hours of direct sun and easy access to water.

  • 2. Start Small

    A 1.2 x 2.4 m bed or a few large pots is plenty for your first season.

  • 3. Prep the Soil

    Loosen the top 20 cm and mix in compost. Healthy soil does most of the work.

  • 4. Choose Easy Plants

    Go for forgiving crops like lettuce, radishes, herbs, cherry tomatoes and zucchini.

  • 5. Plant and Water

    Follow the seed packet spacing and water deeply once the soil feels dry an inch down.

  • 6. Mulch and Maintain

    Add mulch to hold moisture and block weeds, then check on your plants a few minutes each day.
