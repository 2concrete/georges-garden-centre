A small gardening guide

Six steps for getting something to grow.

  • 1. Pick a spot

    Find sun, water and enough room to move around.

  • 2. Start small

    A few pots are easier than a whole backyard.

  • 3. Prep soil

    Loosen it and add compost before planting.

  • 4. Pick easy plants

    Herbs, lettuce and radishes are forgiving.

  • 5. Water

    Water deeply whenever the surface feels dry.

  • 6. Keep checking

    A few minutes each day prevents most problems.
